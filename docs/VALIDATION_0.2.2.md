Murphy independently checked the exact lab.3 predecessor inside the isolated native Hermes on this Mac: play, still sky, mute, 4/4–0/4 availability, invalid time, and minimise/restore pause. Those observations are distinct from the new browser tests. Prior 0.2 native screenshots remain historical 0.2 evidence, not screenshots of the new sky.

No claims of native Windows/Linux testing, low-end hardware performance, complete window-occlusion coverage, native OS-setting reduced-motion changes, or model-driven conversational tool selection. The user listened and approved the track; automated tests do not judge taste, balance or the audible loop seam. Copying a meeting proposal never creates a calendar event. No automatic voiceover plays in the plugin; separate narrated demo media is outside this package.

The older videos do not demonstrate the final music/aurora integration. A new real-Hermes capture is needed before describing these features in a submission video. No video editing, publishing, pushing, catalog merge or competition submission was performed in this integration task.
